# 'FunctionDefinition' and 'ChatCompletionsToolDefinition' name the tool-calling payloads of
# the chat-completions wire contract. The names are fixed by the protocol these types
# serialize to and from and match the equivalent surface in Azure.AI.OpenAI, so renaming
# them to avoid the 'Definition' suffix would make the library inconsistent with both the
# service and its sibling clients.

nowarn:AZC0031 T:Azure.AI.Inference.ChatCompletionsToolDefinition

# The colliding 'FunctionDefinition' is Azure.Deployments.Core.Definitions.Schema.FunctionDefinition,
